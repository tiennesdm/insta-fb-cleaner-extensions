# Privacy Policy for InstaClean & FBClean Chrome Extensions

**Last updated:** November 3, 2026

## Overview
**InstaClean — DM & Message Deleter** and **FBClean — Facebook Messenger Chat Deleter** operate 100% locally inside your web browser. We do not collect, transmit, rent, or sell any personal data, messages, credentials, or browsing activity.

## What Data We Store Locally
- **User Preferences & Settings**: Your chosen cleanup mode, action delay timer, batch cooldown settings, and your list of protected ("Keep") conversation names.
- **Session Statistics**: Counts of deleted, skipped, and errored conversations, along with a local history log so you can review your session progress.

## How Data Is Stored
All preferences and session logs are stored strictly on your own device using Chrome's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). No data is ever sent to any external server, cloud database, or third-party service.

## Third-Party Services & Analytics
These extensions do **not** use any third-party analytics, tracking pixels, cookies, telemetry, or remote servers.

## Data Sharing
No user data is shared with any third parties under any circumstances because no data ever leaves your browser.

## Data Retention and Deletion
All locally stored settings and session logs remain on your device only as long as the extension is installed. You can clear the history log at any time using the **Clear** button inside the extension popup, or remove all stored data permanently by uninstalling the extension from Google Chrome.

If this privacy policy is updated in future versions, the updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
